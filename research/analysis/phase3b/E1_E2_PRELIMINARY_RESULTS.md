# Draft: E1/E2 preliminary Results

A development pilot evaluated 30 scene–question pairs from 10 distinct RSVQA-LR scenes using one fixed synthetic cloud seed and seven target coverage levels (0–60%). Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct produced 1,080 predictions across clean, cloudy and four repair conditions. Integrity checks confirmed complete pairing and preserved image identities. All comparisons below exclude the zero-cloud condition, yielding 180 repeated question-conditions per method; these are not 180 independent scenes.

**E1 — Cloud-affected VQA.** Clean-image exact-match accuracy was 43.33% (13/30). Across nonzero cloud levels, direct cloudy-image inference achieved 49.44% (89/180). Relative to repeated clean predictions, 14 answers changed from incorrect to correct and 3 from correct to incorrect. This development observation does not establish that cloud contamination improves reasoning; it motivates investigation of scene-specific answer instability and model shortcuts. The ten numeric questions had zero exact matches across all evaluated conditions. A secondary normalization check for standalone number words, decimal equivalence and thousands separators recovered no matches.

**E2 — Reconstruction versus no reconstruction.** Predicted-mask Telea, predicted-mask Navier–Stokes, reference-mask Telea and CPU automatic repair achieved 47.22%, 47.78%, 43.89% and 44.44%, respectively, compared with 49.44% for direct cloudy inference. CPU automatic repair recovered 3 answers and damaged 12, a paired change of −5.00 percentage points (nominal scene-bootstrap 95% interval: −11.11 to −0.56 points; 2,000 resamples of 10 scenes). Two questions accounted for 8 of the 12 damage events. Reference-mask repair was not an upper bound: its accuracy was lower than predicted-mask Telea by 3.33 points. The pilot therefore provides no evidence of an average reconstruction benefit under these settings. Its small development sample, shared synthetic cloud seed, annotation ambiguity and exploratory multiple comparisons preclude broad or held-out performance claims.
